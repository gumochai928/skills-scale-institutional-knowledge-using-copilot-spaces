# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers ensure smooth, repeatable deployments and own incident response and rollback procedures. They serve as the gate-keepers for production readiness and coordinate cross-functional go/no-go decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Own and maintain deployment playbooks and release checklists
- Conduct release readiness reviews with Developers and Product Managers
- Coordinate with the Support Lead for go/no-go decisions before each release
- Manage production deployments and monitor post-deploy health
- Lead incident response, rollback execution, and root-cause analysis
- Maintain on-call schedules and escalation runbooks

### Goals
- Achieve zero-surprise releases through thorough preparation
- Minimize mean time to recovery (MTTR) for production incidents
- Continuously improve deployment reliability and automation

### Typical Communication
- Pre-release readiness meetings with Dev and PM leads
- Incident status updates to stakeholders during active incidents
- Post-incident reports shared with the full team
- Deployment announcements and release notes to Support and stakeholders

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts capture and translate business requirements into actionable specifications. They bridge the gap between Product/Project Management and Development by conducting process mapping, user research, and acceptance criteria definition.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, document, and validate business requirements through stakeholder interviews and workshops
- Conduct process mapping to identify inefficiencies and opportunities
- Define and refine acceptance criteria for user stories and features
- Collaborate with Product Managers on solution scoping and trade-off analysis
- Support Developers in understanding requirements and expected behaviors
- Maintain a requirements traceability matrix to track coverage

### Goals
- Ensure development work directly addresses validated business needs
- Reduce rework caused by ambiguous or misunderstood requirements
- Accelerate decision-making by providing clear, structured analysis

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Written specifications, user stories, and acceptance criteria documents
- Regular syncs with Product Managers and tech leads
- Walkthroughs with Developers before sprint work begins

---

## Support Lead

### Role Summary
Support Leads champion the customer experience before and after launch. They coordinate support readiness, gather customer feedback, and drive resolution of high-priority issues by closing the loop between customers, Product Managers, and Development.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and execute support readiness activities ahead of each release
- Create and maintain support documentation, FAQs, and escalation guides
- Collect and synthesize customer feedback and bug reports post-launch
- Prioritize and triage high-impact customer issues in coordination with PM and Dev
- Facilitate the feedback loop so customer insights reach product and engineering teams
- Track resolution status of customer-reported issues and communicate updates

### Goals
- Minimize customer-impacting issues at launch through proactive preparation
- Ensure customer feedback drives continuous product improvement
- Maintain high customer satisfaction and trust throughout the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Pre-launch support readiness reviews with PM and Release Manager
- Weekly bug triage and feedback summaries with Dev and PM
- Customer-facing communications and release announcements
- Escalation reports for critical production issues

---

## UX Researcher

### Role Summary
UX Researchers conduct usability studies, user interviews, and synthesize findings into actionable insights for Product Managers and Developers. They ensure that design and product decisions are grounded in real user behavior and needs.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and execute usability studies, user interviews, and surveys
- Synthesize qualitative and quantitative research into clear, actionable insights
- Collaborate with Product Managers to incorporate user insights into roadmap priorities
- Partner with Developers to evaluate technical constraints against user experience goals
- Maintain a repository of research findings accessible to the full team
- Facilitate design critique sessions and usability feedback reviews

### Goals
- Ground product decisions in validated user insights rather than assumptions
- Improve usability and satisfaction scores across key user journeys
- Create a shared understanding of users across product, design, and engineering

### Typical Communication
- Research planning sessions with Product Managers at the start of each initiative
- Insights reports and synthesis presentations shared with the team
- Collaborative design workshops with Developers and Product Managers
- Regular participation in sprint reviews to connect user feedback to completed work

---

## Information Security Manager

### Role Summary
Information Security Managers assess compliance, data privacy, and security risks throughout the project lifecycle. They partner with Developers and Product Managers to embed secure practices from design through deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct threat modeling and security risk assessments for new features and changes
- Review architectural and technical designs for security and compliance implications
- Define and enforce security requirements, policies, and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate security reviews, penetration testing, and vulnerability remediation
- Ensure compliance with applicable data privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR, SOC 2)
- Advise the team on secure coding practices and participate in code reviews for sensitive areas
- Manage security incident escalation and response in coordination with the Release Manager

### Goals
- Prevent security incidents by integrating security early in the development lifecycle
- Ensure ongoing compliance with regulatory and organizational standards
- Build a security-aware culture across all project teams

### Typical Communication
- Security review sessions during project initiation and architecture design phases
- Risk and compliance status updates in weekly delivery syncs
- Findings reports and remediation tracking shared with Dev leads and PM
- Escalation to sponsors for high-severity risks requiring business decisions
